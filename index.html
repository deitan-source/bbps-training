About This Module

This training module standardises the application of the Boston Bowel Preparation Scale (BBPS) for all gastroenterologists participating in the RIGHT-CLEAN prospective trial. You will learn to score bowel preparation quality consistently and identify patient eligibility for randomisation.

Section 1 of 10

What is the BBPS?

The Boston Bowel Preparation Scale is the world's most widely validated instrument for assessing colon cleanliness during colonoscopy. It evaluates three colonic segments separately, each scored 0–3, yielding a total of 0–9.

🔴
Right Colon
Cecum + ascending colon
0 – 3 pts
🟡
Transverse Colon
Including hepatic & splenic flexures
0 – 3 pts
🟢
Left Colon
Descending + sigmoid + rectum
0 – 3 pts

🏆 Total Score Range

0 = Completely unprepared
9 = Perfectly prepared (best)

Reported as segment scores + total, e.g. 1/2/3 = 6

📋 RIGHT-CLEAN Eligibility

Eligibility is determined by the right colon BBPS at caecal intubation.

Score ≤ 2 (0, 1 or 2) → ELIGIBLE
Score 3 only → NOT eligible

⏱️ When to Score

  • Always score on withdrawal
  • After washing & suctioning each segment
  • Perform all cleaning before scoring
  • Always aim for the highest possible score

🔬 Original Validation

First published by Lai et al., 2009. Inter-rater reliability ICC = 0.91. The most widely used bowel prep scale in research worldwide.

BBPS Score 2 — Eligible Preparation

In the RIGHT-CLEAN trial, scores 0, 1, and 2 are all eligible for randomisation. Only Score 3 excludes a patient. Understanding Score 2 vs Score 3 is the most critical distinction.

The Key Discriminating Question

Is the mucosa entirely visible with no opaque material at all?
NO
→ Score 0, 1 or 2

Some opaque or turbid material present. Score depends on how much and whether it can be cleared.

✅ ELIGIBLE for RIGHT-CLEAN
YES
→ Score 3 only

Entire mucosa seen with no opaque material whatsoever. Clear liquid only (if any fluid).

🚫 NOT ELIGIBLE for RIGHT-CLEAN

Score 2 — Identifying Features

  • Entire mucosal surface well seen throughout
  • Minor turbid or cloudy liquid present
  • Residue fragments that can be fully suctioned away
  • After maximal suctioning: clear view of all mucosa
  • No opaque material at all → that is Score 3 (not eligible)
  • Score 3 = only clear liquid permitted

Clinical tip: Score 2 is NOW ELIGIBLE. If uncertain between Score 2 and 3: is any turbid or opaque residue present? If yes → Score 2 (eligible). Only a completely clear, opaque-free preparation → Score 3 (not eligible).

Section 8 of 10

How to Assign the BBPS

Follow this four-step workflow at every colonoscopy in the RIGHT-CLEAN trial.

1

Reach the Caecum / Terminal Ileum

Complete full colonoscopy to the caecum or terminal ileum as per standard practice.

2

Wash & Suction Each Segment

Perform all cleaning manoeuvres before scoring. Always aim for the highest possible score.

⭐ Record the right colon BBPS at this point — this determines RIGHT-CLEAN eligibility.
3

Score During Withdrawal

Assign 0–3 to each segment: right colon, transverse colon, and left colon independently.

4

Sum the Segments & Document

Total BBPS = right + transverse + left (range 0–9).

Example: Right=1, Transverse=2, Left=3 → 1/2/3 = 6

⚠️ Important: Right colon score at caecal intubation = eligibility determinant. Score 0, 1 or 2 → eligible. Score 3 only → not eligible.
